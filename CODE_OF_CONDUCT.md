# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, constructive, and specific.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Give actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.
- Respect different reading habits, languages, and accessibility needs.

Unwelcome behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and posting copyrighted material without permission.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate these expectations.
